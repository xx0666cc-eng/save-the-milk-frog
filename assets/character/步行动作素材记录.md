## 实际生成提示词

Use case: identity-preserve. Production game walk-cycle sprite sheet on TRUE TRANSPARENT alpha. Reference image is the exact existing game character. Preserve this golden yellow chubby pear-shaped creature, HUGE low belly, seamless head and back, green irises, tiny closed mouth, pale cream belly, olive brown hands and three-toed feet, smooth rendered shading. No redesign, no tail, no costume. Make EXACTLY 24 separate full-body sprites in an EXACT regular grid of 8 COLUMNS and 3 ROWS. Wide landscape sheet. Each cell has identical dimensions, same head size and body scale, feet at identical baseline, character centered on pelvis, generous transparent margins. No text, labels, grid, ground or shadows. Row 1: eight consecutive frames of ONE complete walking cycle facing LEFT in the reference three-quarter side view. Row 2: eight consecutive frames walking TOWARD viewer, front view, arms hanging loosely swinging alternately, belly swaying subtly. Row 3: eight consecutive frames walking AWAY from viewer, back view, rounded yellow back, arms swinging, no eyes or belly visible from behind. For EACH row, columns 1-8 are a cyclic sequence: left-foot contact, weight down, right-foot passing, right knee advancing, right-foot contact, weight down, left-foot passing, left knee advancing, then seamlessly loop to column 1. Legs must actually alternate, heel lifts, knees bend; do not repeat the same stride eight times. Opposite arm swings against the leading leg, hands relaxed rather than resting on belly. Keep camera and proportions identical across frames within each row. These are smooth restrained walking poses, NOT running, not exaggerated lunges. Preserve the exact reference face and body identity in every view.
