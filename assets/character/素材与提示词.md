# 图片角色素材记录

采用内置 ImageGen。两张用户原始参考图片保存在 `../references/standing.jpg` 和 `../references/laugh.jpg`，未经覆盖。玩家用二维图片平面显示；没有从单张图恢复真实三维模型。

- `standing-cutout.png`：依据全身参考图去背景的透明站立素材。AI 抠图可能存在局部细节变化，不保证逐像素一致。
- `actions-sheet.png`：依据同一角色补画的九姿势图集，包含站立、两种迈步、蓄拳、出拳、踢腿、防守、受击和大笑。动作素材是补画，不是从参考照片直接提取已有动作。
- `sprite-data.js`：原 PNG 数据及帧边界的离线封装，供游戏和预览共用。运行不需要外网。
- 动作目前是有限姿势切换和位移，不是完整逐帧动画。后续可根据试玩反馈增加中间帧；本轮完成后停止。

## 站立素材实际提示词

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: attached original full-body yellow plump cartoon creature. Remove ONLY the white background and the ground shadow, producing a genuine transparent RGBA cutout. Preserve the original character exactly: pose, three-quarter view facing LEFT, silhouette, huge low belly, head seamlessly flowing into the back, green irises and black pupils, tiny closed mouth, golden yellow skin, creamy belly patch, olive-brown hands and feet, original smooth rendered lighting and shading. Keep both feet and every finger complete. No redesign, no new pose, no 3D reconstruction, no costume, no added tail, no altered expression, no text. Maintain the original proportions and the original camera perspective; only background becomes transparent. Single full-body character, tightly framed with a small transparent margin.

## 动作图集实际提示词

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset: a single production fighting-game SPRITE SHEET, exactly 3 columns by 3 rows of nine equally sized SQUARE cells on transparent RGBA background, preferably 3072x3072 total. Image 1 is the exact character identity and body to preserve; image 2 provides ONLY the laughter expression for final cell. Every cell shows the SAME full-body character, SAME fixed three-quarter camera facing LEFT, SAME size (height approximately 80% of cell), feet on the SAME baseline 92% down its cell, adequate transparent padding and no part outside its cell. Smooth original cinematic 3D-render appearance. Preserve huge pear-shaped belly, seamless rounded head/back, oversized GREEN eyes with black pupils, tiny mouth, golden yellow skin, pale cream belly, dark olive fingers and three-toed feet. No costume, hair, accessories, tail, redesign or text. Exactly ONE separate full character per cell. Read cells left to right, top to bottom: row1: (1) original resting hands-on-belly pose; (2) walking left, left leg forward right leg back and arms naturally swinging; (3) opposite walking step. Row2: (4) punch anticipation, fist pulled back near chest; (5) fully extended punch toward LEFT, extended hand a closed fist, visible reach, other hand near belly; (6) front kick to LEFT at belly height, one foot firmly on ground. Row3: (7) defensive blocking with both forearms up near face and chest; (8) recoil being hit, leaning backward to RIGHT with surprised face; (9) laughing full-body with hands clutching belly and tightly closed crescent eyes, open wide mouth with teeth matching image 2, retaining image 1 body proportions. NO labels, NO grid lines, NO shadows outside the character, NO floor, NO gray or checkerboard background. Actual alpha transparency. Identity consistency is critical; borrow the source appearance rather than stylizing it.

图集实际输出为 1254 × 1254，站立素材为 928 × 1694；提示词中的期望尺寸不代表实际输出尺寸。
