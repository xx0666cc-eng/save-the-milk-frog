# 金纹武士·持剑

使用内置 ImageGen，参考原始服装 reference.jpg 与旧 atlas.png 补画持武器动作。新图片为 atlas-weapons-v2.png，旧版本保留。8 列 × 5 行，共 40 姿势；站立、三方向步行、攻击、防守、受击与跳跃均携带武器。data-weapons-v2.js 封装原 PNG 字节及帧范围，没有改写图像像素。实际游戏中武士使用剑斩，游侠使用飞行箭矢。

## 实际提示词

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit/rebuild a production 2D game sprite atlas, TRUE transparent RGBA with alpha-zero blank space, exactly 8 columns x 5 rows, 40 separate full-body sprites. Image 1 is the exact outfit and character identity to retain. Image 2 is the existing game sprite atlas: retain its chubby body, head scale, outfit details, rendered style and 8x5 layout. Change the character's hands/arm poses to actually wield the weapon described below. Keep yellow skin, green eyes, tiny mouth, round belly; do not make a human. Same armor/clothes/hat/boots and accessories throughout. Smooth shaded 3D-rendered raster, not pixel art.
Every cell equal size with generous transparent margins. Entire weapon tip, headgear, hands and feet inside the cell, no overlap into neighbors. Fixed character scale/pelvis baseline and camera per view; large props may extend horizontally but body size stays constant. No text, grid lines, motion streaks, ground, shadows, colored backdrop, or detached flying projectiles. All side attack poses face LEFT, so the weapon aims left. Weapon remains visibly held in walking, jumping, guarding, recoiling, victory and dash poses. NO empty fists as the attack; NO kicks. Exact row contents are given below.
ROW 2: 8 successive phases of one smooth WALK LEFT cycle, three-quarter side view. The weapon is carried in hand. Alternate legs: left contact/down/right passing/right knee forward/right contact/down/left passing/left knee forward. Armor/cape follows lightly; no duplicate strides.
ROW 3: 8 successive phases walking TOWARD camera, front view, weapon held.
ROW 4: 8 successive phases walking AWAY camera, rear view, weapon still held in front/at side as visible from rear. No face on back.
Final asset is the actual atlas only on transparent background. Identity and weapon continuity are critical.
WEAPON: ONE drawn polished steel slightly curved sword matching the reference's black/gold hilt, visibly gripped in hand, blade exposed. Empty scabbard remains at waist. Never a second sword, never put the held sword back into sheath. Every attack is a sword technique.
ROW 1 left to right: (1) idle ready stance holding drawn sword in front toward LEFT, (2) sword slash windup pulled back, (3) horizontal sword cut LEFT with blade extended, (4) low diagonal finishing sword cut LEFT, (5) sword across body to parry, (6) hit recoil still gripping sword, (7) airborne with sword in hand, (8) landing with sword kept safely forward.
ROW 5: (1) rising upward sword cut, (2) forward sword thrust/cut launching a blade wave but do not draw the wave, (3) powerful overhead sword finishing slash toward LEFT, (4) laughing victory raising sword, (5) fallen defeated with sword beside/gripped hand, (6) front-facing idle holding blade, (7) back-facing idle holding blade, (8) forward dash carrying sword. Preserve dark navy/gold floral armor, red cords, golden horned helmet.
